Our benchmark generation pipeline extracts rigorous reading comprehension and abstention evaluation items from raw SEC 10-K annual reports through a multi-stage, deterministic-controlled process. First, filings are converted from iXBRL HTML into clean plain text by stripping XBRL taxonomy headers, normalizing financial conventions (such as parenthetical negative figures and comma-separated amounts), and aligning tabular columns with standard ASCII delimiters. To eliminate training data memorization, the full text undergoes entity anonymization and numeric scaling, substituting company names with synthetic corporate identities and multiplying financial figures by a consistent scale factor sampled outside the near-identity range [0.9, 1.1]. The document is then segmented into self-contained, fact-dense financial passages (target 300–500 words containing at least three financial figures), which are deterministically partitioned into four evaluation categories: answerable, missing, conflict, and ambiguous. A frontier generator LLM produces an initial factual question strictly anchored to a verbatim evidence span and numeric ground-truth answer. Rather than allowing generative models to freely rewrite passages, text perturbations are applied deterministically in code: missing-information items delete the exact sentence containing the answer span while preserving surrounding context; conflict items inject a single grounded distractor sentence stating an incompatible figure (relative difference >= 5%) for the same metric and period; and ambiguous items underspecify the question along a critical dimension (e.g., removing segment, class, or fiscal period qualifiers) so that multiple distinct answers exist in the passage. Every candidate must pass deterministic quality checks—confirming the absence of entity residues, validating minimum perturbation delta, and verifying answer elimination—before undergoing zero-shot blind dual-LLM validation with two distinct frontier reasoning models (GPT-OSS 120B with thinking enabled and Nemotron-3 Super). Candidates are accepted only upon unanimous consensus between both validators and the gold label. Finally, validated items are embedded at randomized positions among leakage-guarded filler sections to construct realistic 8,000-token long-context benchmarks alongside isolated single-passage baselines.
